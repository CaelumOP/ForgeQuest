# CC BY-SA 4.0 notice for exercise illustrations

Some exercise thumbnails in `app/src/main/assets/ex_media/` and `web/public/ex-media/` are adaptations
of illustrations released under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International licence
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/, full legal code: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode).

Which move uses which picture is listed in `tools/catalog/media_sources.json` (bucket `oea` and `workout-guide`).

## Adaptations

Our WebP files are adapted: resized to at most 360 px, converted to WebP, and composited onto the app's dark
card colour (#1E1F20) instead of a transparent background. These adaptations are themselves licensed
CC BY-SA 4.0. The licence applies to the illustrations only, not to the rest of ForgeQuest.
